# Artemis.Views

Group `3_Domain` - pure static library - what one agent can know, built from the omniscient world.

## Responsibility
Builds the affordances of the local player (what it can pick up, enter or use) and one `View` per row of `Players` for a set of agents
(F4.1: the head and Self, F4.2: the aim hit and the rays, F4.3: who sees whom, the other players and the flags, the sounds and notices empty until F4.5 and F4.6). The View of the watched player is what
the dev tools draw and compare.

## Does not
- Take part in the Tick with `Oracle`: `Views.Oracle.*` is the pure comparison, counters and report text of the raycast oracle (R3, D272), a development tool. It lives here only because it needs `RaycastHit` and `Artemis.Tests` cannot reference `DevTools.*`. `Views::Layer`, the services and the rest of the pipeline never import it, only `DevTools.Workbench` does.
- Decide actions: that is the Python agent.
- Build the action: that is `Control`.
- Flatten anything for Python: that is `Bridge.Codec`.

## Input and output
- Input: the stores of `Perception::Layer`, `Blam::Runtime::Layer` and `Blam::MapModel::Layer` (world and raycaster).
- Output: `AffordanceStore` and `ViewsStore`, members of `Views::Layer`, one published value per tick. `RaycastSceneStore` is also a member, but it is state between ticks and not an output.

## Public interface
Other projects may import only these modules; everything else in the project is internal.

| Module | What it gives |
|---|---|
| `Views.Layer` | The facade: stores and services; `Cleanup()` forgets the map. |
| `Views.View.Type`, `Views.View.State`, `Views.View.System` | The View of an agent (D307-D321) and the `AgentSet` that says who gets one. `ViewTable` is one `View` per row of `Players`, in the same order (`k_MaxPlayers`): `ViewHead` (player handle and row, `LocalIndex`, `BipedHandle`, `Mode`, `IsComputed`, `HasBody`, `Team`, `Side`), `ViewSelf` (eye, feet, heading and pitch of the aim, velocities, vitality, shield, ground, crouch, zoom, respawn, powerup, grenades, ability, `CarriesFlag`, `TicksSinceDamage`, `IsTracked` and the two `ViewWeapon` slots), `ViewAimHit` and `Rays` (`k_ViewRayCount` 16, F4.2), `Others` (one `ViewOther` per player) and `Flags` (2, F4.3), and the sections that later tasks fill and that are zero now: `Sounds` (`k_ViewSounds` 8) and `Notices` (`k_ViewNotices` 8). `AgentSet` is a mask by player table index (the low 16 bits of the player handle) that enters `PipelineService::Run` as data, with the `ViewMode` of every View of the set (`Mode`, HumanLike by default, D321). `ViewsService::Update(agents)` publishes `ViewsStore`: a computed View for each player of the set, a View of zero bytes for any other row. For each agent with a body it casts the aim ray from `Eye` along `Body.Aim` (range: the autoaim of its primary weapon, capped at 150, looked up by the `DatumIndex` of the weapon so the tag name is read once per kind of weapon, 50 without one) and the rays of the pattern (`Views.Raycast.Type:Pattern`, generated from `Data/Views/Rays.xml`, D311) from the bounding center of the vehicle it rides or of its biped, in the frame of the aim (D311): forward is the heading of `Body.Aim` on the ground plane and up is the world up. Against the frame of the forward of the object that the first version used, 6987 of 51072 perception rays of the local player on ctf_d (13.7%) gave another result. `AimHit` and each ray carry the distance (`MaxDistance` or the aim range on a miss), the kind, the surface flags, the material of a static hit and, for a dynamic hit, the object handle and the `Function` of its entity (the aim hit also the point, the range, the subtype, the region index and the `PlayerRow` of the biped, `0xFF` if it is not a player's). `View.RayOrigin` is where the rays of the pattern start. `FindObservedView(views, handle)` gives the View of the watched player (the handle, else the one with `k_PrimaryLocalUser`, null if neither is computed), `RaycastsOf(view)` rebuilds the `Raycasts` the dev tools draw and compare (the aim ray from the eye along `AimDirectionOf`, the pattern from `RayOrigin` in the frame of the aim) and `ViewPositionOf(view)` is the eye, or the feet when the eye is zero, `ObsTargetsOf(view)` is where the two arrows of the observation go (VW.17: feet to the enemy flag `Flags[1]` and to the own stand `Flags[0].StandPosition`, nothing without a computed View or body, none for a team without side, a mirror of `ObserveBatch` in ctf.py) and `RivalTeamOf(objectives, team)` the team that owns the rival stand. `Relation` compares the team of the agent with the team of the entity hit (Neutral if either has none or the entity is unknown, None without a dynamic hit). `IsHead` stays false: the collision region names are not at hand, only `RegionIndex`. The scene is prepared once per Update (`PrepareScene`, when the first agent with a body needs it) and the exclusions of the 16 players come from one pass of `BuildGraphExclusions` over the object graph. A dead agent has no rays. Self copies `Players`, `Entities` and `WeaponDetails` and the eye is `DerivedEye` (D309), zero when the biped has no tag. A dead player keeps `RespawnTicks`, the powerup and `Side` and has no body: handles `0xFFFFFFFF`, no zoom, as in the bridge. `Side` is the `BaseTeam` of the stand whose `OwnerTeam` is the team of the player in this round (the lowest handle if there are several), `Neutral` when it owns none. `IsTracked` is a locked `TrackedObject` of any weapon equal to the biped or to the vehicle it is seated in. Others and Flags (F4.3): after the rays of every agent `Update` runs one sight pass for the whole set (`ComputeSight`, D310). `Head(p)` is the derived eye of every living player with a biped definition, also of the ones outside the set. A pair of living players within `k_SightRange` is a candidate when at least one of the two is an agent in HumanLike mode that has the other inside its cone (`k_SightHalfAngle` around its `Body.Aim`). One ray goes from head to head over the prepared scene, ignoring what both players carry or ride, and serves both directions: `Seen(a, b)` is `b` in the cone of `a` with the line free. `RaycastScene.SightCandidates` and `SightRays` hold the candidates and rays of the last Update (0 when it did not run). In Full mode `Seen` is every living player but the agent, with no cone and no ray. `Others[i]` is the player of `Players[i]`: `Relation` is Self, Ally, Enemy (two valid teams, the same or not) or None (a team that is not valid, or a row past the table). The `Hud` bit goes to every ally, which always has `IsAlive`, `CarriesFlag` and, while alive, `Position`. The `Seen` bit fills the whole row: `Velocity`, `FacingYaw` (heading of its `Body.Aim`), `Vitality` and `Shield` (NaN when it has none, as in Self), `WeaponSubtype` (of the equipped weapon) and `IsCrouching`. A row without channel is zero but for its `Relation`, so an enemy that carries a flag and is not seen is only in the Flags. The `Radar` bit and `Elevation` stay zero until F4.6. `Flags[0]` is the flag whose `OwnerTeam` is the team of the agent in this round and `Flags[1]` a rival one (the lowest handle if there are several), always and with no channel (D113): a copy of `ObjectiveDetails` where `Position` is the one of the biped of its carrier when carried and the one of its own entity otherwise, `CarrierRow` the row of `Players` of the carrier (by player, else by biped, `0xFF` without one) and `StandPosition` the origin of its home stand. `HasFlag` says whether the flag exists in this tick (`State` 0 is Home, so a zero row cannot tell): while the engine recreates a flag (reset, return, capture) `ObjectiveDetails` has none, and then the row has `HasFlag` false and only `StandPosition`, the origin of the stand of the side (D370): `Flags[0]` the stand whose `OwnerTeam` is the team of the agent, `Flags[1]` one of a rival team (the lowest handle if there are several, the rule of `Side`), the rest of the row zero. A team without side (`Neutral`), or a side without stand, leaves the row zero. Others and Flags are filled for every agent of the set, a dead one included. The Views Each View also has `Interaction` (F5.1 A, D371), after `Self`: the one object the engine would have the agent use (`ViewInteraction`: `Kind`, `Detail`, `TargetHandle`, and the `Function` and `Subtype` of that entity in `Entities`, Unknown and 0 when the target is not there), read from the selection of the action applied to the agent (`InteractionStore::AcquireSelections`, by the index of the handle). A computed View without selection says `None` / `None` / `0xFFFFFFFF`: a dead agent, an agent without an action, and every agent in a tick without published actions (the region `PlayerActions` is empty then), which is also how the Views of a recording made before the region read. A View outside the set is all zero. It is the prompt a human sees on the HUD (D113), not an advantage. The engine arms it from the world before the tick, so it describes the objects of the previous tick. The Views cross the bridge as the `BridgeView` of `Contract` (F4.7, D369), a copy of these types with the same fields in the same order: a change of a field, an enum value or one of the constants (`k_ViewRayCount`, `k_ViewFlags`, `k_ViewSounds`, `k_ViewNotices`, `k_ViewWeaponSlots`) goes to `Data/Bridge/Bridge.xml` in the same commit, and `Bridge.Codec.Flat.System` fails to build when the values drift. |
| `Views.Affordance.Type`, `Views.Affordance.State` | What the agent can interact with, and how. |
| `Views.Raycast.Type`, `Views.Raycast.State` | The pattern of the perception rays (`Pattern::k_PatternRays`: yaw and pitch in degrees in the frame of the agent, positive yaw to the left, and the range; the rows are the cube of 14 rays, six faces and eight corners, 20 wu). ## State between ticks
- `RaycastSceneStore` (`Views.Raycast.State`): the two level ray scene (a hierarchy per collision mesh of the map and two trees of instances, FIXED and MOBILE) and, by handle, the parts of every object with the `CollidablePartsKey` they were built from and `StillGathers`, the gathers in a row with the same key (0 in the gather where it changes or appears). An object whose key is the same keeps its parts without collecting them again (D299), one that is gone leaves. The key of an object is brought up to date in its entry, in place, from the row of the table the gather already holds (D301): no lookup per object and a field is copied only when it differs. The `SceneObject`s of a gather view the vectors of those entries (`std::span`), they do not copy them. An entry is replaced only for an object whose key changed, before its `SceneObject` is made, and only entries of objects not in the result are erased, so the views are valid until the next gather: nothing keeps a `SceneObject` past the arming and casting of its tick (the trees and `FixedPoses` hold their own copies of the poses). It is used on the AI thread. The boxes of the instance trees are the posed box of each part cut with the box of its sphere (RK.1, D306). `GatherSceneReach` collects once per agent the pieces that rays from one origin within a range can touch and `CastSceneRayReach` casts through them (same hit as `CastSceneRay`, which it falls back to for another origin, a longer ray, another exclusion or a scene armed again since the gather: the reach keeps the sorted handles of its exclusion and a `SceneStamp` of the scene, RV.1). A ray whose origin, direction or range is not a number misses in `CastScene` and `CastSceneRayReach`. `ViewsService` uses it for the perception rays. `PrepareScene` gathers the objects and arms both trees once per `ViewsService::Update`.
- Rest and the fixed tree (D300). A gather marks an object as at rest if its placement is Fixed or Phased and its key did not change since the last gather (or it is new), or if `StillGathers` reached `k_RestGathers` (8). A change of key takes it out of rest in that same gather. `ArmFixedScene` keeps the FIXED tree: an object in it that is not at rest with the same poses any more goes to a sorted filter of handles that casts skip, and its current parts go to the MOBILE tree, without building. An object at rest that is not in the tree waits in the MOBILE tree. The tree is built again with every object at rest and an empty filter when something waits or is filtered and either it was never built, or `k_FixedRebuildGathers` (32, the least spacing) gathers passed since the last build and there are `k_FixedRebuildPending` (16) waiting (objects at rest not in the tree plus filtered handles) or the wait has lasted `k_FixedRebuildMaxGathers` (512) gathers (D301, `FixedRebuildRule`, `RaycastScene::FixedWaitingGathers` counts the gathers in a row with something waiting and starts over when nothing waits). What waits lives in the MOBILE tree and the filter, so the hits do not depend on when the tree is built. The MOBILE tree is armed in every gather from the objects the FIXED tree does not cover, so the parts of an object are always in exactly one of the two. When its pieces (handle, mesh and part index, in order) are the same as in the last arming only the poses changed, and `ArmMobileScene` keeps the topology and refits the boxes from the leaves up (`RefitBoundsBvh`, D302): the leaf boxes are the spheres of the parts, so it holds exactly what a build would and only the quality can get worse. It builds again when the pieces change, or when the area of its inner nodes after the refit is above `MobileRebuildRule::MaxArea` (`k_MobileRebuildArea`, 2) times the area it had when it was built. `RaycastScene` keeps `MobileBuiltArea`, `MobileAreaRatio` and the counters `MobileBuilds`, `MobileRefits` and `MobileDegraded`, and is emptied with the rest of the scene. The constants count gathers: one per `ViewsService::Update`. The tie of two hits at the same distance goes by handle, part and triangle, which does not depend on the tree. `Cleanup()` runs on the lifecycle thread, so it only marks the store, and the next use empties it all: the hierarchies of the meshes point into the collision models of the map, so nothing of an old map may be used. The first tick after a load builds the hierarchies of the meshes (about 14 ms once).
- Every other store holds only the value of the last tick.

## Threads
Updates run on the AI thread, called by `Bridge.Codec.Pipeline.System` after `Perception`. Nothing blocks.

## Errors
An agent without a unit (dead, not spawned) gets an empty view for that tick.

## Requirements
- The affordances are those of the primary local player (`k_PrimaryLocalUser` of `Common`), the Views are one per agent.
- Deterministic and pure, like the rest of the pipeline.
- Helpers of the views per agent (F4.B, used by `ViewsService` since F4.2), pure functions of `Views.Raycast.System`, internal to the project and measured by the offline bench (`DISABLED_RaycastBench`, table M6): `BuildGraphExclusions(players, entities, nodes)` builds in one pass over the players the exclusion of every row (biped, loadout if alive, children of the biped in the object graph, root of the vehicle and its vehicle children). `FindVehicleRoot` finds the root of the vehicle a biped rides. `DerivedEye(biped, definition, crouchFraction)` is the eye A: the world origin plus the up vector times the camera height of the bipd tag, from standing to crouching; `HeadBoneEye(bones, headNode)` is the eye B: the node of the head, invalid if the matrix is not valid. Neither needs a camera, so they hold for bots (`DISABLED_ViewEyeTests.DerivedEyeMatchesLocalCamera`, 2-3 min, measures them against the camera of the local players).
